Privacy Policy

This policy explains how Cronos Edital handles information in the app and related services.

1. Scope and controller

This Privacy Policy applies to the Cronos Edital application and directly related public pages. Cronos Edital is responsible for processing performed directly by the application. Third-party services such as Google AdMob, Google User Messaging Platform, Google Play and GitHub Pages process data under their own policies, agreements and legal roles.

The app currently prioritizes local operation and does not require a Cronos Edital account to store study data on the device.

2. Information that may be processed

  • User-provided data: study and organization information, including exams, notices, questions, flashcards, sessions, notes and imported content.
  • Technical app and device data: app version, operating system, platform, device characteristics and technical identifiers when required for compatibility, diagnostics and security.
  • Network data: IP address and ordinary technical metadata transmitted when accessing remote catalogs, external links, advertising services, app stores or other online services.
  • Advertising and consent data: privacy choices handled by Google UMP and data Google Mobile Ads/AdMob may process, such as advertising identifiers, IP address, device characteristics, diagnostics and ad interactions.
  • Purchases and subscriptions: when Premium purchases or subscriptions are available, the app store may provide product information, purchase/subscription status and transaction identifiers required to unlock entitlements. Cronos Edital does not receive complete payment-card details.

3. Local storage

Study and configuration data are stored locally on the device by default. They remain until changed or deleted by the user, removed through app features, cleared by the operating system, or erased by uninstalling/clearing app data, subject to platform backup behavior where applicable.

4. Advertising, Google AdMob and UMP

Free users may see ads served by Google AdMob / Google Mobile Ads. Before requesting ads, the app uses the Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) to check consent status and, where required, display a privacy message.

Depending on region, Google configuration and the user's choice, ads may be personalized or non-personalized. Cronos Edital does not request ads while the SDK reports that ad requests are not yet allowed.

Where required, an “Ad privacy” option lets users revisit choices supported by the platform.

Google services: see the Google Privacy Policy and Google advertising information.

5. Premium features and payments

Cronos Edital may offer a Premium tier without ads and with expanded limits. When purchases or subscriptions are enabled, financial processing is handled by the applicable app store, such as Google Play. The store collects and processes payment data. The app may receive only information required to confirm, restore, renew, cancel or expire Premium entitlement.

6. Purposes

  • provide, maintain and personalize app functionality;
  • save content and preferences on the device;
  • import, retrieve or update content requested by the user;
  • support compatibility, integrity, security and abuse prevention;
  • manage consent and ads for Free users;
  • manage Premium entitlements and purchases when available;
  • diagnose errors and improve stability and performance;
  • comply with legal, regulatory and rights-protection obligations.

7. Legal bases

Where applicable, processing may rely on bases provided by Brazil's LGPD and, for people covered by the GDPR, corresponding bases under European law. Depending on context, these may include performance of requested functionality, consent, legal obligations and legitimate interests related to security, fraud prevention and technical operation.

8. Sharing and third parties

Cronos Edital does not sell personal data. Information may be processed by third parties when necessary for a requested feature, including Google AdMob/Google Mobile Ads, Google UMP, Google Play or another applicable store, content/infrastructure providers and GitHub Pages for hosting this policy.

When this page is visited through GitHub Pages, GitHub may log the visitor's IP address for security purposes under its own privacy practices.

9. International transfers

Global providers such as Google and GitHub may process data in other countries. Such processing is subject to the mechanisms, contracts and safeguards adopted by those providers and applicable law.

10. Retention and deletion

Data stored only on the device remain for as long as needed for app use and can be removed through app features, clearing app data or uninstalling, depending on the data type. Third-party data follow the retention periods and controls of the respective providers.

11. Privacy rights

Depending on applicable law, users may have rights of confirmation, access, correction, portability, deletion, information about sharing, objection, restriction and withdrawal of consent. Some rights can be exercised directly on the device where data exist only locally.

Requests concerning data processed directly by Cronos Edital can be made through the support channel shown on the app's official store listing or other official project channels. Requests concerning third-party controlled data may also need to be directed to those providers.

12. Security

Technical and organizational measures appropriate to the nature of the app are used, including operating-system isolation, integrity checks and secure connections to remote services where available. No system is completely immune to failure.

13. Children and minors

Cronos Edital is not specifically directed to children. Parent or legal-guardian supervision is recommended for minors, especially for advertising, purchases or external services.

14. External services and links

The app may open third-party pages, documents or services. Processing after accessing those services is governed by each provider's own privacy terms.

15. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated to reflect changes to the app, third-party services or law. The version and date above identify the current revision.

16. Contact

For privacy questions, use the support channel shown on the official Cronos Edital app-store listing or the project's official channels.
